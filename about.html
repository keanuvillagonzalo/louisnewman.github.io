A passion for helping college students succeed

Louis Newman is a College Success Coach, Educational Consultant, and Author of Thinking Critically in College: The Essential Handbook for Student Success.

Connect With Louis

Louis Newman is the former Dean of Academic Advising and Associate Vice Provost for Undergraduate Education at Stanford University. He is also a professor emeritus of religious studies at Carleton College, where he taught and advised students for 33 years.

Throughout my career—as a college professor, adviser and administrator—my goal has been to help college students thrive. I know from experience that every student, given the proper support, can transition smoothly to college and use these years to grow intellectually and personally.

I get to know each student personally, so I can help them set goals, overcome obstacles to success, and make healthy decisions. Each student gets the attention they deserve, and the consistent, seasoned advice they need to thrive.

I work with students from all backgrounds and at all stages in their college careers.

Students come with a wide range of needs and goals:

  • Some want to learn to study more effectively and get more from their courses.
  • Some are struggling with personal issues (health or family situations) that can lead to lower academic performance.
  • Some need help with specific decisions, such as how to prepare for interviews, choose a major, or navigate academic policy.
  • Some are trying to decide whether to transfer or take a leave of absence.
  • And some come with neurodiversity, time management, or executive function issues that are preventing them from doing their best work.

With the right support, all these students can achieve their goals and make the most of their college years.

You gave your student all the help they needed to get into college. Now you can give them the help they need to succeed.
