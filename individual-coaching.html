Student's Needs

This personalized coaching adapts to your student's unique schedule, learning style, and goals throughout the entire semester. Louis creates a structured, supportive environment that helps them build confidence and stay accountable.

Your Student's
Personal Action Plan

Coaching can be scheduled to suit your student's specific needs. Most students appreciate ongoing support throughout the semester or for the entire academic year, which gives them essentially unlimited access to my services. Others with more focused needs work with me on an hourly basis. You decide what level of support your student needs.

Either way, coaching involves developing a relationship with your student. There is no fixed number of sessions, and no issue the student can't discuss.

While conversations with students are held in confidence, parents receive a general summary of the coaching work I've done at the end of each semester. And, of course, I welcome parents' input throughout the coaching relationship.

All coaching students and parents are provided with a coaching agreement, which spells out how I will work with your student.
