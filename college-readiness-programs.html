College Readiness Programs

Transitioning to college requires more than just showing up on move-in day—it requires a proactive academic mindset. These focused, short-term programs are built to assess your student's preparation and develop essential skills before they even step foot into a lecture hall.

The College Launchpad provides a focused, one-time readiness assessment to eliminate the guesswork of the freshman transition. Through a detailed survey and a dedicated one-on-one session, we create a custom written plan that highlights their strengths, predicts potential friction points, and identifies vital campus resources.

For a deeper academic foundation, the Ready, Set, Think Bootcamp offers a four-week course designed to build rigorous academic habits. Working directly from my book, we tackle reflection exercises that equip your student with the concrete reading, writing, research, and critical thinking tools needed to succeed at the college level.

College Launchpad: College Readiness Assessment and Transition Plan™

A one-time readiness assessment. Your student completes a detailed survey about their preparation and life experience, then spends an hour with me going deep on what it turned up. I follow that with a written plan: where they're genuinely well-prepared, where they may run into friction, and specific campus resources worth connecting with before they even arrive on campus.

Ready, Set, Think: College Prep Bootcamp™

A four-session course built directly from my book, Thinking Critically in College. Over four weeks, we work through the book's reflection questions and exercises together — one-on-one, or in a small group of friends. By the end, your student will have concrete tools for reading, writing, research, and thinking at a college level.
