Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to College Success Coaching. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you how we handle the information you provide when you interact with our website and outlines your privacy rights.

2. The data we collect & how we collect it

We collect minimal personal data to facilitate our services. Specifically, we collect your name and email address when you voluntarily provide it to us through:

  • Website contact forms
  • Newsletter sign-ups
  • Our appointment scheduling platform (Calendly)

Additionally, we utilize Google Analytics and cookies to automatically collect anonymized technical data (such as browser type, site navigation patterns, and generalized location) to help us analyze website traffic and improve your browsing experience.

3. Data sharing and third parties

We value your trust and privacy. We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties.

This exclusion does not apply to trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business (such as Calendly for managing bookings and Google Analytics for website analytics). These tools are solely used to facilitate our operations, and those providers are bound by their own strict confidentiality and privacy agreements.

4. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the removal of your data from our systems, please contact us at:

coach@thinkingcritically.us
